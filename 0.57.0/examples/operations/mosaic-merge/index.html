    Mosaic & merge¶

    Combine several rasters into one:

    • merge_rasters(src, dst) — mosaic many (overlapping or adjacent) rasters into a single raster covering their union.
    • stack_bands(files, path=...) — stack several single-band rasters into one multi-band raster (e.g. assembling per-band Sentinel files into one image).

    Notes¶

    • merge_rasters takes no_data_value= and method= to control fill and overlap handling.
    • stack_bands can align=True to snap mismatched grids before stacking.
    • See also: Reproject / resample / align.
